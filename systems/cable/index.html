Original product information

Details worth knowing.

Mesh coverage

The company’s Anti Pollution System uses mesh for general hauling where weather protection is not the priority, including dirt, sand and gravel.

Vinyl coverage

The published Semi-Waterproof System uses vinyl for weather resistance and heat retention. Discuss the load and temperature requirements for asphalt or other sensitive materials; this is not a waterproof guarantee.

Built around the body

Published bow options include 13-inch and 18-inch arches, with custom arches available by request. Reinforced bow pockets and an optional aluminum or diamond-plate wind deflector are described on the company site.
