Precision Tarp started in 1997, when Sisombath Philavong founded a small sewing business in his garage.

Today, that family-owned business manufactures and installs tarps for dump body trailers, serving the Chicagoland area from its home in Elgin.

A few helpful answers.

Can you help me find the right replacement part?

Search the published part numbers above, or add an identification request to your quote. Include the system type, any markings and your truck details. Confirm compatibility with the team before ordering.

What information do I need for a custom tarp?

Start with your box dimensions, material being hauled and existing tarp system. The team can confirm final measurements, material and attachment requirements before manufacturing.

Do you offer installation?

Precision Tarp’s company website describes tarp manufacturing and installation. Contact the team to discuss your vehicle, location and scheduling.

Can I pay for an order here?

This website preview prepares a quote request. It does not accept payments or place orders. Current pricing, availability, delivery and order terms must be confirmed directly with Precision Tarp.
