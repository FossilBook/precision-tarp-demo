03 / PREPARE YOUR REQUEST

Measurements with context.

Truck or trailer system

  1. Record the vehicle or body make/model and existing tarp-system label, if available.
  2. Note body length in feet and width in inches. Label exactly where each measurement was taken.
  3. Describe the load, operating preference and existing mount. Include any visible obstructions or clearance limitations.
  4. Ask the team which additional measurements are needed before selecting or installing equipment.

Custom tarp or cart curtain

  1. Sketch the box or opening and label each side. For curtains, identify each cart opening separately.
  2. Distinguish opening/body dimensions from the desired finished cover dimensions. Include units for every measurement.
  3. Mark the side drop, overlaps, attachment points and closure arrangement you need.
  4. Include quantity and preferred material/color, then confirm the final specification with the team.

This is an inquiry checklist, not a fabrication template. Measure only when equipment is safely parked and accessible; have the installer verify hard-to-reach dimensions.
